## 2. Micro-CRO Behavioral Tactics Reference

1. **Flashing Logo for Gmail:**
   - *Technique:* 2-frame looping animated GIF brand avatar or Gmail Promotion Card hero thumbnail to trigger peripheral visual motion detection.
   - *Result:* 18%–32% lift in open rates.
2. **Dual Cart Reservation & Delivery Promise Clock:**
   - *Copy:* *"Your cart is reserved for 14:59 minutes"* + *"Order within 2 hrs 14 mins to receive it by Tuesday, Nov 18"*.
   - *Result:* Eliminates delivery anxiety while activating loss aversion.
3. **Asymmetrical Decoy Sizing Arbitrage:**
   - *Pricing:* Small size at 45 AED (\$25), Large size (2.5x volume) at 50 AED (\$28).
   - *Result:* 80%+ of volume shifts to the larger size, dramatically lifting AOV.
4. **Behavioral Price Typography (The Zero & Dot Elimination Rule):**
   - *Format:* Write **`$49`** or **`49 AED`**, never **`$49.00`** or **`49.00 AED`**.
   - *Result:* Fewer syllables and characters reduce phonological friction in working memory, making the price feel physically and psychologically lighter.
