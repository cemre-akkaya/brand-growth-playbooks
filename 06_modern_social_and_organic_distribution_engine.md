# Playbook 6: Modern Social & Organic Distribution Engine (B2B & D2C)
**Version:** 2.0 (2026 Production Standard)  
**Objective:** Build a high-converting organic distribution engine that captures category mindshare, drives inbound customer acquisition, and establishes brand authority across both D2C (Instagram, TikTok) and B2B (LinkedIn, YouTube).

---

## 1. The Expert Mindset: B2B vs. D2C Bifurcation

| Vector | D2C Social Strategy | B2B Social Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Platforms** | TikTok, Instagram Reels, Pinterest, YouTube Shorts. | LinkedIn, X (Twitter), YouTube Long-form. |
| **Account Anchor** | Brand aesthetic page + Creator/Influencer whitelisting. | Founder / Executive personal profiles (People follow people, not company logos). |
| **Buying Trigger** | Emotional aspiration, sensory desire, visual problem/solution. | Career advancement, risk reduction, operational ROI, peer status. |
| **Conversion Path** | Link in Bio $\rightarrow$ Advertorial / Landing page $\rightarrow$ One-click checkout. | Post $\rightarrow$ Comment lead magnet $\rightarrow$ Direct Message / Calendar booking. |

## 2. Profile Architecture: What to Pin & How to Optimize

Your social profile is not an art gallery; it is a **high-converting landing page**.

### A. D2C Profile Architecture (Instagram & TikTok)
1. **Profile Picture:** Clean, high-contrast product shot on solid background, or founder face with direct eye contact.
2. **The 3-Line Bio Formula:**
   - *Line 1 (The Transformation):* What the product does in plain English.
   - *Line 2 (Social Proof):* *"Trusted by 45,000+ customers"* or *"Featured in Vogue / Forbes."*
   - *Line 3 (The CTA):* *"Shop the new collection 👇"* + Clean trackable URL.
3. **The Exact 4 Pinned Story Highlights (Must Have):**
   - **Highlight 1: "Start Here / The Story"** — Founder video explaining the origin, the problem with traditional brands, and the brand's core values.
   - **Highlight 2: "Results / Proof"** — Unfiltered customer UGC, before/after transformations, lab reports, and press mentions.
   - **Highlight 3: "How To Use"** — 30-second tutorials, unboxing rituals, and sizing/usage guides that eliminate buyer friction.
   - **Highlight 4: "VIP / Drops"** — Limited-edition releases, loyalty perks, and secret community discount codes.
4. **The 3 Pinned Grid Posts:**
   - *Pin 1:* The Brand Manifesto Video (Who we are & what we stand for).
   - *Pin 2:* The Best-Selling Hero Product Deep-Dive (Why it works).
   - *Pin 3:* The Viral Social Proof / Celebrity or Press Endorsement.

### B. B2B Profile Architecture (LinkedIn)
1. **Banner Image:** Custom branded banner with a 1-sentence value proposition + logos of notable clients or media appearances.
2. **The Headline Formula:** `I help [Target ICP] achieve [Specific Metric/Outcome] without [Hated Friction] | [Current Role] at [Company]`
3. **The 3 Pinned Featured Section Items:**
   - *Featured 1:* High-value Lead Magnet (A free diagnostic calculator, spreadsheet model, or 20-page SOP).
   - *Featured 2:* Signature Case Study (Detailed breakdown of how a client generated \$1M+ or solved a major operational bottleneck).
   - *Featured 3:* The Founder's Most Viral Teardown Post (Establishing industry contrarian authority).

## 3. Aesthetics, Visuals & Music Strategy: What Actually Works

### Visual & Lighting Standards
* **D2C (Instagram / TikTok):**
  - *Lighting:* Clean, high-key natural light or diffused softbox lighting (never direct harsh flash unless deliberately shooting vintage 90s flash aesthetic for apparel).
  - *Framing:* 9:16 vertical, eye-level framing. Use macro close-ups for textures, ingredients, and tactile unboxings.
  - *Visual Pacing:* Dynamic cuts every 2.0 to 2.8 seconds to sustain sub-conscious attention.
* **B2B (LinkedIn / YouTube):**
  - *Visual Style:* Clean, minimalist workspace or daylight desk setup.
  - *Graphics:* High-contrast markdown tables, hand-drawn Excalidraw diagrams, or clean 1080p screen recordings showing actual dashboards and code.

### Audio & Sound Design
* **D2C Sound Architecture:**
  - *Music Selection:* Use trending low-tempo rhythmic beats, neo-soul, or lo-fi electronic instrumentals. Align cuts directly with the beat drop.
  - *ASMR Foley:* Amplify tactile sounds (the "click" of a bottle cap, the opening of a zipper, the tear of packaging). ASMR increases watch time by 35% on TikTok.
* **B2B Sound Architecture:**
  - *Voice-First:* Crisp, professional microphone audio (Shure SM7B or DJI Mic) with zero background noise.
  - *Background Music:* If used, duck subtle lo-fi ambient instrumental to **-24 dB to -28 dB** below the vocal track. Never let music overpower the spoken thesis.

```markdown

- Target Platform: [SPECIFY: TikTok/Instagram OR LinkedIn/X]
- Target ICP: {{TARGET_ICP}}
- Core Product / Service: {{PRODUCT_OFFER}}
- Founder / Brand Voice: [e.g., Candid, Contrarian, Scientific, Minimalist]

Deliver a complete, production-ready blueprint following this 4-step framework:

### STEP 1: Profile Architecture & Pinned Content Strategy
1. **Bio & Header Optimization:** Write the exact Bio copy, Headline, and Link-in-Bio call-to-action.
2. **Pinned Strategy:**
   - If D2C: Define the exact title, cover aesthetic, and content script for the **4 Core Story Highlights** (Story, Proof, How-To, Drops) + the 3 Pinned Grid Posts.

Define the aesthetic standards tailored to this brand:
- Lighting and camera setup (e.g., Lo-fi iPhone vs. Studio macro).

- Sound Design & Music Strategy (trending audio vs. lo-fi ambient vs. ASMR).

### STEP 3: The 30-Day Content Matrix (10 Core Concepts)
Develop 10 specific content concepts mapped across:
- 4 Authority / Contrarian Posts (Tier 1)
- 4 Tactical Utility / How-To Posts (Tier 2)
- 2 Human / Culture Posts (Tier 3)
For each, provide: The Angle, The Hook, and The Format.

### STEP 4: Full Production Execution (3 Masterpiece Posts)
Write 3 complete, ready-to-publish posts/scripts:
1. One 45-second Short-Form Video Script (with Visual Scene Direction, Spoken Audio, and Music cues).
2. One High-Authority Text Teardown Post.
3. One Step-by-Step Educational Carousel / Guide.
```
